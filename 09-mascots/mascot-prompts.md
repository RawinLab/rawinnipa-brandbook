# Rawinnipa Mascot Character Sheet Prompts

> Use these prompts for gpt-image-2 or equivalent high-quality image generation.
> Brand rule: mascot visuals may use lotus pink/green as character colors, but Rawinnipa corporate identity remains dark canvas with Rawin Green as a sparse accent.

## Master Style

Use case: logo-brand / stylized-concept
Asset type: mascot character sheet for brandbook, sticker system, chatbot avatar, slides, social media, and course materials
Brand: Rawinnipa Software and Consultants
Brand personality: progressive, dependable, collaborative, clear

Overall style: polished 2D vector-friendly cartoon mascot design, sticker-ready, crisp edges, smooth rounded forms, expressive faces readable at small sticker size, premium cute but credible for B2B software consulting. Keep the design clean and reproducible.

Visual language: modern Thai tech brand. Use subtle contemporary Thai cues: wai gesture, gentle Thai smile, simplified lotus-petal geometry, minimal geometric Thai line motifs. Avoid sacred, royal, religious, ceremonial, or costume-heavy references.

Brand palette: Deep Black #0A0A0A background, Charcoal #1A1A1A, Graphite #2A2A2A, Off White #F7F7F7, Ash #9CA3AF, Rawin Green #3EDC81 used sparingly as a tech accent, optional tiny Deep Teal #23B591 glow.

Character sheet composition: one full-body hero pose centered, with multiple smaller pose thumbnails around it. Include front view, side or three-quarter view, head/avatar crop, and 6-8 sticker-ready poses. Use generous spacing and clear separation. No readable text, no logo, no watermark.

Common sticker poses: wai greeting, waving hello, thinking with document, explaining with cursor, celebrating solved task, calm warning/help gesture, sending file, debugging/fixing, thank you, tired but still polite, idea moment, holding a digital node.

Avoid: photorealism, 3D toy render, heavy traditional costume, chada, temple imagery, religious symbols, royal symbols, anime school uniform, childish baby look, clutter, excessive green, readable text, actual company logo, watermark.
