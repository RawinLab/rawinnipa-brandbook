# Rawinnipa Mascot System

> Mascot directions for Rawinnipa brand communication, LINE stickers, chatbot avatars, course slides, social posts, and training materials.

## Role

The mascot system gives Rawinnipa a warmer, more expressive brand layer without replacing the logo. Use mascots when the communication needs a human or playful touch: onboarding, training, chatbot states, stickers, workshop slides, help messages, social posts, and light merch.

The core idea is **Thai digital consultant + lotus companion**:

- **Female consultant** — primary human mascot direction. Best fit for the Rawinnipa name and B2B trust.
- **Male consultant** — supporting consultant direction for alternate scenes or team-based stories.
- **Green lotus** — brand-color lotus companion, closer to the corporate palette.
- **Pink lotus / Nong Dok Bua** — warmer, more sticker-friendly lotus companion.

## Usage Rules

- Mascots are supporting brand characters. They do not replace the Rawinnipa logo.
- Keep the default brand environment dark: Deep Black `#0A0A0A`, Charcoal `#1A1A1A`, and sparse Rawin Green `#3EDC81` tech accents.
- Pink is allowed only as a mascot palette for Nong Dok Bua. It is not a new corporate primary color.
- Use subtle Thai cues: wai gesture, gentle smile, simplified lotus-petal geometry, and minimal geometric Thai motifs.
- Avoid sacred, royal, religious, ceremonial, or costume-heavy Thai references.
- Keep expressions clear at small sticker size.
- For final sticker production, regenerate or crop pose-level assets on transparent background instead of using the whole character sheet.
